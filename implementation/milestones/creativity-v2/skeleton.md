# Creativity v2 — sparse combinatorial seeds

## Intent

Creativity will help an operator explore a problem through small, sometimes
awkward combinations of ideas. The model first supplies subjects or other
foci and a shared collection of actions or transformations. The genetic
search chooses which pairs participate and in what order. The evaluator
develops each combination into a complete proposal and judges that proposal.
Strange or unsuccessful combinations are useful evidence, not defects to
hide. The frozen operator mandate remains the completion authority.

This milestone owns that change inside the existing Creativity feature:
admitting material, evolving combinations, evaluating them, continuing saved
work, and showing the operator what happened. Existing tasks keep their
original interpretation. The surrounding task service, execution controls,
prompt selection, staffing and accounting continue to serve the feature.
The feature proposes ideas; it does not act on them.

The experiment compares literal subjects and verbs with more abstract foci
and transformations on literary, strategic and ordinary planning problems.
Mechanical correctness and the usefulness of model proposals need different
evidence. A small, inspectable comparison is sufficient; this is not a new
research platform or a promise that either vocabulary will be more creative.
Each slice gets its detailed design and acceptance checks when it starts.
