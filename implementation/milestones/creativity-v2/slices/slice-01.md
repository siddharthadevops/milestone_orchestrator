# Slice 1 — Admit shared search material with durable semantics

## INTENT

This slice gives the Creativity operator one collection of subjects or other
foci and one shared collection of actions or transformations. Every focus can
use every action. The operator can supply this material or ask the model to
create it; supplying usable material avoids paying for its creation. The
original request and the material's explanation of the problem remain separate
from the choices that the search will eventually vary.

The slice owns admission, removal of repeated values, compatible creation
instructions and the saved distinction between new and old tasks. Earlier
tasks retain their meaning, including tasks stopped before their material was
created. Once material has been admitted and saved, reopening the task uses
that material again. A model may still supply an unimaginative or poorly
grounded repertoire; admitting its structure does not certify its quality.

Non-goals are choosing participating genes, generating or identifying
candidates, scoring proposals, changing stopping rules, adapting displays,
and comparing creative quality. Those belong to the other assigned slices.
This work does not add a service, dependency, importer, editor or recovery
system. It depends on the existing task admission, prompt selection, call
accounting and local persistence owners.

Acceptance ends at the saved material handoff: equivalent generated and
supplied inputs yield the same admitted material, supplied material needs no
creation call, and reopening preserves both material and interpretation.
Sparse search execution is a later integration dependency, not acceptance
evidence for this slice. The main risks are accidentally changing old tasks,
serving creation instructions for the wrong representation, and giving equal
values different identities. Focused admission, prompt and restart checks
address those risks without requiring a live creative-quality experiment.
