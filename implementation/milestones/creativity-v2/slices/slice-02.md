# Slice 2 — Evolve participation and effective ordered seeds

## INTENT

This slice lets the Creativity operator explore combinations that use some
of the admitted ideas rather than requiring all of them. The existing search
can change which ideas participate, what each contributes and their order.
A larger collection of possible contributions must not make omission rarer.
Different hidden arrangements that present the same combination count as the
same work, so they cannot consume extra evaluations or inflate what remains
to explore.

It owns the search handoff: producing combinations, describing their visible
content, recognizing repeats, preserving structural variety and determining
whether any unused combination remains. It depends on the preceding slice's
admitted shared material and saved distinction between new and historical
tasks. Historical searches keep their interpretation.

Non-goals are material admission, evaluator instructions, scoring and validity
policy, task-loop integration, saved-run continuation, stopping policy,
operator displays and live creative-quality comparisons. Acceptance is a
working search contract that those consumers can use, with focused local
tests; it is not an end-to-end sparse run. No new service or package is needed.

The main risks are making participation depend on repertoire size, mistaking
invisible arrangements for new work, changing historical behavior and
underestimating the size of the search space. Controlled variation cases and
small exhaustive examples address correctness. They do not promise a useful
idea, a particular mix in every population or fast exhaustion of a huge space.
