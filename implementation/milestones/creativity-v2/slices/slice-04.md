# Slice 4 — Run scored evolution over a fixed repertoire

## INTENT

This slice gives the Creativity operator a complete run of the sparse search.
It connects the admitted ideas, evolving combinations and evaluator already
built by the preceding slices. The search keeps using the same collection of
ideas throughout a run. It chooses survivors using the scores of whole
proposals, while retaining structural variety. An unsuccessful combination
does not disqualify its ingredients from appearing in a better combination.

The slice owns running the search, keeping its evidence, producing its final
results and continuing saved work. Weak or invalid proposals remain visible
with their assessments, including when they are the best the run found.
Stopping and reopening a task preserves accepted work. Historical tasks keep
their original interpretation.

Dependencies are the preceding slices' material, identity and evaluation
contracts and the existing task controls, local storage and accounting.
Non-goals are new genetic operators, new evaluation instructions, material
admission changes, control/catalogue redesign, panel presentation and the live
creative-quality comparison. Existing result readers receive the compatibility
changes needed to read this slice's output; richer displays remain separate.

Acceptance requires focused tests of a complete run, scored selection,
retained results, exact stopping and continuation, alongside historical
regressions. The main risks are hiding paid work, evaluating the same visible
combination twice, counting unfinished work as completed and changing old
tasks. Controlled replies and small exhaustible examples establish mechanical
correctness. They do not establish creative usefulness or fast exploration of
a large search space.
