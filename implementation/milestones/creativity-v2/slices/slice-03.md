# Slice 3 — Materialize and evaluate the exact seed

## INTENT

This slice gives the Creativity operator a complete proposal developed from
the combination the search actually chose. Every participating idea must
matter to that proposal, in the chosen order. The evaluator should make the
strongest proposal that combination supports and judge it against the same
problem and criteria used for every other candidate. Awkward combinations
remain useful, inspectable evidence.

It owns the evaluator's input, instructions, accepted answers and the record
of which evaluator produced them. Changing the evaluator later must not
discard or charge again for already accepted work. It depends on the saved
distinction between sparse and historical tasks, the admitted shared material
and the preceding slice's ordered active combinations. Historical evaluation
keeps its original interpretation.

Non-goals are changing material admission or genetic variation, choosing
survivors, integrating the complete evolution loop, changing stopping rules,
building operator displays or running the live creative-quality comparison.
Acceptance is a usable evaluation handoff with focused local checks, not a
completed sparse task from start to finish.

The main risks are evaluating ideas the search omitted, silently replacing a
weak combination, scoring relative to its batch mates and losing accepted
work when settings change. Local tests can establish what was sent and saved.
They cannot prove that a model used every idea causally or that scores from
different calls are equally well calibrated; those judgments remain visible
in the retained proposals and explanations.
